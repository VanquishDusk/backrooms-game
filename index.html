The Backrooms

You've been here before.

Somewhere past the edge of the ordinary, there is a place that keeps what the world throws away. Empty offices, dead malls, hallways nobody walks. Rooms that hum. It goes on further than it should, and it is never quite empty.

Mind where you step. If you're not careful, you noclip out of reality in the wrong spot, and end up here.

HOW TO SURVIVE

  • Find a flickering wall and throw yourself through it. It is somewhere different every time. Listen for its static.
  • Your sanity drains in the hum. Darkness, red rooms and things you shouldn't see drain it faster.
  • Drink Almond Water to steady your mind.
  • When the buzzing spikes, cover your ears and keep moving until you're out of it.
  • The halls shift when you aren't looking. Don't trust your memory.
  • If you see a dark figure, don't stare. If it follows you, run.
  • Your backpack holds 4 items. A lead pipe or a road flare stuns what's chasing you for a few seconds; it won't kill it.
  • Watch your step near the Pitfalls.
  • Where the buzzing quiets and the carpet dries, the rooms look half-built. There may be a door.
  • Level 1: when the lights die, something hunts the halls. Get into a corridor. Crates hold supplies, junk or worse. To leave, follow the pipes.
  • Level 1.3 (through a white wall in Level 1): Detox Zones heal you, unless the Decay got there first. Never step into the black.
  • Level 1.5 (through a fake window in Level 1): quiet whispers mean something is close. Don't look away from her. Follow the hum out.
  • Level 2 (follow Level 1's pipes): narrow tunnels, many dead lights, whole sections that black out. Keep your flashlight ready. Nearly every door is locked; the odd ones aren't.
  • Level 3 (through Level 2's red door): low passages make you crouch or crawl. Sanctums and Base Gamma are safe; entities won't follow you in. Don't touch sparking wires or stand in the purple. Find the old freight lift.
  • Level 4 (Level 3's lift): a safe, empty office. Fill up on Almond Water at coolers, vending machines and fountains. Don't look out of windows that aren't blacked out. If you lose sight of an exit, it may move.
  • Level 5 (Level 4's stairs and elevators): the Terror Hotel. Deathmoths circle the lights. The grand stair goes back up. Elevators go to other floors, one to Level 3; never ride to 13. Eat at the Homely Hotel and keep some meat for the cat. The boiler room's pipes carry Almond Water, but the deeper you go the hotter it gets.
  • Level 6 (stairs by Level 5's furnace): total darkness, and your lights won't work. Keep moving; listen for waves to find the stairwell. Don't run over the wires. Wrong turns can close behind you.
  • Level 7 (the stairwell in Level 6): don't go near the doorway unless you mean to. In the sea you swim where you look; hold C / Space (UP) to rise. Watch your breath and your warmth. Find the boat: steer it, and throw harpoons (click, R or THROW) at the serpent's head when it surfaces. Stay away from Fort Surrender.
  • Level 601 (the neon EXIT door in Level 4): you can't die here, and you can't leave. Find the computer that's awake and let it type; get away from the Partygoers. Find the vending machine in the field. When the host comes, keep walking, and in the hallway follow the piano.
  • Level 94 (escape Level 601): safe while the music plays. When it winds down, get inside a lit house before night. Drink at the fountain; take Almond Milk from the vans. In the castle, don't touch things while a Robo-Man is watching; find the invitation, and pass the King's test.
  • Level 8 (Level 7's underwater cave, or a pool in Level 6): it's dark and your batteries die fast. Drink from the water (E). Follow the Light Guides, then the 9th Road's stakes. Cover your mouth in the gas. When the water rises, climb.
  • Level 9 (the end of Level 8's 9th Road): stay on the sidewalk, especially in the fog. Search the houses for supplies. Don't stare at the tall figures in the yards. Follow the arrows on the street signs.
  • Level 10 (off the walkway in Level 9's grass fields): safe, mostly. Don't stand still on the soil for long. Drink from the lakes; search the barns. Follow the dirt tracks a long way for Level 11.
  • Level 10.2 (push into the hay in a Level 10 barn): never touch a hay bale; if the Hayruler screams, run. Keep away from the cows, and out of the flower patches. Talk to the Harvesters, but never go into their hut. At night, jump into a puddle (Level 4), or find the bale that fades in and out (Level 10).
  • Level 11 (follow Level 9's arrows, or Level 10's tracks): safe while you leave things alone. Search the open shops; drink from their taps. Touch a Lucky Crane. Every odd shop, alley, grate and bus is a one-way exit; the shrubs are Level 63.
  • Level 12 (a too-white window in Level 11): read the notes as you sit. Sit a quarter turn of the clock, try the door, go through a copycat door out in the white, then try the door again after exactly one turn. Don't wander far.
  • Level 11.3 (a red-windowed building in Level 11): keep moving and don't get cornered. Look at the Servants to freeze them, but don't let a pack bunch up round you. Don't listen to the Ambassadors. Find a mask. Head for the waterfront: an unbroken bridge leads back to Level 11.
  • Level 13 (a white staircase in Level 12): eat from the fridges, but don't settle in: watch the COMFORT bar and get back out to the halls. Plain unnumbered doors lead to Level 0; the lifts go down a long way.
  • Level 14 (sometimes, through Level 4's windows, or falling from Level 63): it's beautiful. Don't listen. Cover your ears, follow the sound of water and stay near it. Don't stand still among the guests, and don't stare at the children.
  • Level 15 (a white door in Level 14's forest): safe, but there's no Almond Water. Talk to Enric. Read the computers: three files fit together into a map to the door with no symbol over it. It's the way out.
  • Level 63 (a rare metal door in Level 5): high in the sky and completely safe. Sit at a Meditation Point to clear your head. Monkey bars only hold as long as your stamina. Falling isn't fatal; you wake up somewhere else.
  • Photograph an entity to add it to the Field Guide. Earn stickers for little goals. Try Tonight's Tape: one seed a day, the same for everyone.
  • Press V (or the camera button) to take a photo. Your photos become the covers of your tapes in the menu's Archive.
  • Level 0.2 falls apart once you're inside. Find the door back to Level 0 before the collapse reaches it. Cover your mouth in the dust. Outlets charge your flashlight.

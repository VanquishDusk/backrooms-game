HOW TO SURVIVE

  • Find a flickering wall and throw yourself through it. It is somewhere different every time. Listen for its static.
  • Your sanity drains in the hum. Darkness, red rooms and things you shouldn't see drain it faster.
  • Drink Almond Water to steady your mind.
  • When the buzzing spikes, cover your ears and keep moving until you're out of it.
  • The halls shift when you aren't looking. Don't trust your memory.
  • If you see a dark figure, don't stare. If it follows you, run.
  • Watch your step near the Pitfalls.
  • Where the buzzing quiets and the carpet dries, the rooms look half-built. There may be a door.
  • Level 1: when the lights die, something hunts the halls. Get into a corridor. Crates hold supplies, junk or worse. To leave, follow the pipes.
  • Level 1.3 (through a white wall in Level 1): Detox Zones heal you, unless the Decay got there first. Never step into the black.
  • Level 1.5 (through a fake window in Level 1): quiet whispers mean something is close. Don't look away from her. Follow the hum out.
  • Level 2 (follow Level 1's pipes): narrow tunnels, many dead lights, whole sections that black out. Keep your flashlight ready. Nearly every door is locked; the odd ones aren't.
  • Level 3 (through Level 2's red door): low passages make you crouch or crawl. Sanctums and Base Gamma are safe; entities won't follow you in. Don't touch sparking wires or stand in the purple. Find the old freight lift.
  • Level 0.2 falls apart once you're inside. Find the door back to Level 0 before the collapse reaches it. Cover your mouth in the dust. Outlets charge your flashlight.

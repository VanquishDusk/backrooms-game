# The Backrooms

A replayable 3D horror game set in the Backrooms, playable in the browser on desktop and mobile.

Playable now: **Level 0 — "The Lobby" / "The Yellow Hell"**, its sub-level **Level 0.2 — "The Remodel"**, **Level 1 — "The Habitable Zone"**, **Level 1.3**, **Level 1.5 — "Inverted"** and **Level 2 — "Abandoned Utility Halls"**. More levels will follow.

## Play

You can serve the folder with any static file server. There's no build step.

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then open http://localhost:8000
```

It also works on GitHub Pages: enable Pages for the branch and open the site URL.

## Level 0

You've noclipped out of reality into endless mono-yellow halls, damp carpet and buzzing fluorescent lights.

- **Find the flickering wall** and throw yourself through it. It's in a different place every run. Listen for its static.
- **Sanity** drains in the hum. Darkness, the red rooms and things you shouldn't see drain it faster.
- **Almond Water** steadies your mind.
- **Buzzing spikes:** the lights get painfully loud. Cover your ears and keep moving until you're out of it.
- **The maze shifts** where you aren't looking.
- **Whispers, distant voices, scratching behind the walls, footsteps that aren't yours.**
- **A dark figure** watches from the end of the corridors. If your sanity slips far enough, it stops watching.
- **The Pitfalls:** a room full of holes in the floor. Watch your step.
- **Dark zones** where the lights died (use your flashlight), and a **red room**.
- **Exit:** throw yourself through the flickering wall to reach Level 1.

Some layouts have a pocket where the buzzing quiets, the carpet dries out and the rooms look half-built. If you find the **door** there, it leads to Level 0.2.

## Level 0.2 — The Remodel (Survival Difficulty 3)

Level 0 as remodelled by the Backrooms Remodeling Co. It has dry red carpet, fresh white paint, working outlets and the odd bathroom. A few seconds after you arrive, it starts falling apart.

- A **chain reaction** spreads out from where you came in. Ceiling tiles fall, walls topple and the floor caves in to show the unfinished house underneath. Debris hurts you.
- **Dust** chokes you. Cover your mouth (same button/key as covering your ears) and get out of it.
- Find the **door back to Level 0** before the collapse reaches it. Level 0's hum leaks through it, and there's light under the door. If the collapse gets there first, there's no way back.
- **Outlets** charge your flashlight.
- Going back puts you in a fresh Level 0. Your sanity, health, battery and Almond Water carry over.

## Level 1 — The Habitable Zone (Survival Difficulty 1)

An endless, abandoned concrete complex: half underground car park, half empty warehouse.

- **Halls:** huge pillared spaces with damp floors, exposed pipes and long sightlines. This is where you explore, and where things happen.
- **Corridors:** concrete hallways with offices and infirmaries. They're safer, and red emergency lighting stays on in a blackout.
- **Storage:** shelving and crates.
- **Sectors:**
  - **Aquila:** the garage where you arrive, with an explorers' cache and a note.
  - **Gild:** warehouse, full of crates.
  - **Gothic:** vaulted round columns and a medical station.
  - **Ouroboros:** construction that never ends, with workers who never look at you.
  - **Garden:** overgrown and harmful to stay in, but with better loot.
  - **Fabled:** old wood panelling and neon.
- **Blackouts:** the lights die and something hunts the halls. It won't follow you into the corridors.
- **Impossible geometry:** halls stretch away from you, and corridor walls move when you aren't looking.
- **Crates** hold Almond Water, batteries, first aid kits, junk, or something worse. Some vanish when you look away, and new ones turn up.
- **Exit:** follow an extended hallway where pipes and dials gradually appear. Pipes on the ceiling get denser as you get closer, and you can hear steam ahead. Toward the end it gets more industrial: false walls step in at 45 degrees, the ceiling drops, and fat lagged pipes, gauges and caged bulbs crowd in until you walk into Level 2.

Near the explorers' cache, a spotless **white wall** leads to Level 1.3.

## Level 1.3 — The Ward (Survival Difficulty 0… or a Dead Zone)

One long, blinding-white tiled hallway with branching paths to 16 large rooms: Detox Zones (marked in green tape), medical supply rooms, and The Ward. Each visit rolls which Level 1.3 you get:

- **The sanctuary:** Detox Zones heal you quickly (it burns), M.E.G. doctors staff The Ward, the supply rooms are stocked, and heart monitors beep.
- **The Dead Zone:** the Decay has got in.
  - Black voids eat into the floors, walls and ceilings, and they keep spreading. Step into one and you're gone.
  - Dried blood, covered bodies, a flatlined monitor.
  - The Detox Zones heal you for a moment, then tear you apart.
  - Recovered documents tell what happened.
  - One end of the hallway is already being swallowed.
- **Exit:** noclip into either end of the main hallway to return to Level 1.

## Level 1.5 — Inverted (Survival Difficulty unknown)

Reached through a **fake window** set into a concrete corridor wall in Level 1. Through the glass you can see a hallway in negative.

- **The look:** a house hallway shown as a monochrome photo negative.
  - Ceiling fixtures radiate darkness, and the dark ends of the hallways glow white.
  - Picture frames and door trim shine, and the floor at your feet is black.
  - Walls lean and sway, and the camera sits slightly tilted.
- **Upside-down architecture:** chairs and tables hang from the ceiling, fixtures are set into the floor, some doors are hung upside-down with the knob near the top, some pictures are upside-down, and there's backwards writing on the walls. Every so often the whole view turns over, and the controls adjust so they still feel right.
- **Silence:** there's no hum. Denizens drift through the halls whispering, and their whispers get **quieter as you get closer**. If one passes through you, you lose sanity and end up somewhere else.
- **She** only moves while you aren't looking at her.
- **Logs** from the explorer who got trapped here are scattered on the floor, garbled.
- **Exit:** the only hum here is Level 0's, leaking through the one place reality is thin. Follow it to a glitching dead end where the negative keeps failing, and noclip through. You usually land in Level 1, sometimes Level 0.

## Level 2 — Abandoned Utility Halls (Survival Difficulty 2)

Reached by following Level 1's pipe hallway until it narrows into a maintenance tunnel.

- **The tunnels:** a labyrinth of narrow (about 2 m) concrete maintenance tunnels with long straight runs, branches, loops and dead ends.
  - Turns are cut at **45 degrees**, so the tunnels bend instead of meeting at square corners.
  - Walls are chalky, peeling whitewash over board-formed concrete, with rust runs and damp. Some sections are old dark brick.
- **Pipes everywhere:**
  - Every wall carries a bundle that runs continuously along the tunnel, follows each bend and turns corners. It only dives into the wall or rises into the ceiling where the wall ends.
  - Bundles mix fat cream-lagged pipes, rusty iron, red-painted pipe, galvanised pipe, conduit and cable, all on strut-channel brackets.
  - Some walls have a raised concrete ledge instead.
  - Ceilings carry pipe runs, big spiral ducts or cable trays on threaded-rod hangers. There are valves, flanges, gauges and steam leaks.
- **Plant rooms:** small rooms with diamond-plate floors, boilers, pumps, tanks, generators and switchgear. You can hear them whirring from the tunnels.
- **Clutter:** drums, gas bottles, cable spools, cardboard boxes, toolboxes, rubble, paper and broken fluorescent tubes. Wall signs say DANGER, HIGH VOLTAGE or KEEP CLEAR, or give section codes.
- **Light and dark:**
  - Lights are harsh bare fluorescent tubes or orange caged bulbs. Many are dead, some flicker, and there are long dark gaps between them.
  - Some sections are almost entirely dark.
  - Every so often a whole section loses power at once and stays dark for a while.
  - Darkness wears on your sanity, so use the flashlight.
- **Sound:** fluorescent buzz, motors and pumps, water hammer knocking down the pipes, distant clanks, drips and steam.
- **Entities:** Hounds, Smilers (only in the dark), Skin-Stealers, Deathmoths (around the lights, and your flashlight) and Clumps (waiting in dead ends).
- **Doors:** steel maintenance doors sit at the back of dead ends and in plant-room walls. Almost all are locked. A rare few are unlocked, and each looks and sounds different:
  - **Faded green with a chalk "1"**, under a lone bulb, with water dripping nearby. It leads back to **Level 1**.
  - **Rust red with hazard tape and a lightning bolt**, with a pulsing red lamp and arcing electricity. It leads on to **Level 3** (coming soon).
  - **Sometimes, a wooden office door with a brass "4"**, with warm light spilling under it and a scrap of carpet in front. It leads to **Level 4** (coming soon).
- **Supply crates** sit in some dead ends: Almond Water, batteries, first aid, junk, or a burst of steam.

The main menu has a **Levels** section (0, 1, …) and a **Sublevels** section (0.2, 1.3, …). Each level unlocks there once you reach it.

Every run comes from a seed, which shows on the pause, death and escape screens. Type a seed on the main menu to replay a layout.

## Entity library

Reusable entities live in `src/entities/lib/`. Level 2 uses Hounds, Smilers, Skin-Stealers, Deathmoths and Clumps; the rest aren't placed in a level yet. Each has its own model, procedural skin, animations, sounds and AI (idle, movement, detection, attack), and they're lit by the level's lighting and your flashlight.

| Entity | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Smiler** | Glowing eyes and a grin, only in the dark. Drifts closer while you're in the dark with it, lunges if you linger or shine a light on it, then vanishes. |
| **Hound** | A hairless human body on all fours. Hunts by sound (sprinting carries far) and sprints in bursts. Stare it down while standing still and it hesitates. |
| **Faceling** | Ordinary people with blank faces. `passive` ones stop and watch you; `hostile` and `child` ones stalk, then chase. |
| **Skin-Stealer** | Looks like a lost wanderer, waving and calling for help. Get close, turn your back, or wait too long, and it sheds its disguise. |
| **Deathmoth** | Dog-sized moths that circle lights and get drawn to your flashlight. `drone` mostly flutters around you; `soldier` dives at you. |
| **Clump** | Fused limbs and flesh. Hides in tight spaces, lunges when you pass, then drags itself after you. |
| **Duller** | A washed-out grey figure that fades when still. Unpredictable moods; its touch festers (health drains for a while). |
| **Partygoer** | Yellow, smiling =). Dances, waves, invites you over, then the smile tears wider. |
| **Wretch** | Skinless, hunched, lopsided. Twitches and shambles until provoked (proximity or light), then charges in a zig-zag. |

To use them in a level:

```js
import { EntityManager } from '../../entities/lib/index.js';
// in the level constructor
this.entities = new EntityManager({ scene, world: this.world, audio: ctx.audio, rng: this.rng, shared });
this.entities.spawnFar('hound', player, { min: 15, max: 30 });
this.entities.spawn('faceling', { x, z, variant: 'hostile' });
// in update(dt, player)
const res = this.entities.update(dt, player, ctx);
if (res.dead) ctx.onDeath(res.cause);
// in dispose()
this.entities.dispose();
```

Open **`entities.html`** to inspect them. Pick an entity, switch poses (idle, move, detect, attack) and variants, toggle the lights and your flashlight, or turn on **Live AI** to let it come for you (the camera). Drag to orbit, and scroll or pinch to zoom.

## Controls

| | Desktop | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD / arrows | Fixed stick in the bottom-left corner |
| Look | Mouse | Drag anywhere else |
| Run | Shift | RUN (toggle) |
| Cover ears / mouth | C / Space (hold) | EARS / MOUTH (hold) |
| Flashlight | F | LIGHT |
| Drink Almond Water | E | Bottle button |
| Pause | Esc | Pause button |

The Settings screen has mouse and touch sensitivity, FOV, volume, invert Y, graphics quality and an FPS counter. On "Auto", graphics quality steps down if the device can't keep up.

## Project layout

```
index.html, css/style.css     UI shell, menus, HUD, touch controls
vendor/three.module.min.js    three.js r169 (MIT, vendored, no CDN needed)
src/main.js                   entry point
src/core/                     engine: Game loop/state, Input, Player, AudioEngine (procedural), Renderer (+post FX), rng, storage
src/world/                    reusable grid-level toolkit: GridMap (layout + pathing + line of sight),
                              GridWorld (meshes, collision, baked lightmap, destructible cells),
                              materials (shaders), textures (procedural), props (doors, clutter, fixtures),
                              tubes (fast pipe/box geometry writer, chunked meshes)
src/entities/                 Stalker (Level 0's dark figure), Hound (Level 1's blackout hunter)
src/entities/lib/             reusable entity library: Entity base (AI helpers), humanoid rig, skins, 9 entities, EntityManager
entities.html                 entity viewer
src/levels/registry.js        list of levels + the level interface
src/levels/level0/            Level 0 generator and runtime (events, hazards, exits)
src/levels/level0_2/          Level 0.2 generator and runtime (the collapse)
src/levels/level1/            Level 1 generator and runtime (sectors, blackouts, crates, the hallway out)
src/levels/level1_3/          Level 1.3 generator and runtime (Detox Zones, The Ward, the Decay)
src/levels/level1_5/          Level 1.5 generator and runtime (the negative, the denizens, She)
src/levels/level2/            Level 2 generator, service planner (pipes, 45-degree corners, collision) and runtime
```

All textures and sounds are generated in code, so there are no asset files.

## Adding a level

1. Create `src/levels/levelN/LevelN.js` exporting a definition `{ id, number, name, title, difficulty, next, create(ctx) }`. `registry.js` documents the instance interface.
2. Grid-style levels can reuse `GridMap` and `GridWorld` with their own generator, textures and lighting.
3. Register the level in `src/levels/registry.js`.

Levels hand off with `ctx.onEscape(targetId, 'noclip' | 'door' | 'walk')`, and player stats carry over. An exit that targets a level that isn't built yet shows a "coming soon" screen, using the names in `UPCOMING`. Level 2's odd doors already target `level3` and `level4`.
